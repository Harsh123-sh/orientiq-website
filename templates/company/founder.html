About the Founder

Building with
Purpose

ORENTIQ is shaped by a belief that technology is most valuable when it makes the real work of a business clearer, simpler, and more capable.

With a focus on software and AI, the work begins with the problem—not the platform. Each engagement is an opportunity to understand a business workflow, identify where thoughtful technology can help, and build something that is useful long after launch.
